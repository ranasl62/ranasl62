About

I work across academic methods and industrial systems. My research program develops leakage-aware evaluation, probability calibration, and explainability for clinical-risk and imaging models, released as peer-reviewed papers, a methods book, and the open EHR Risk Framework. In industry I design and ship retrieval-augmented generation, multi-step agents, MCP-connected tools, chat interfaces, and automated document/task pipelines—with the same discipline around grounding, tracing, and maintainable software.

Healthcare outputs are research and education methodology, not a medical device and not bedside deployment. Volkswagen employment is production engineering capacity; independent scholarship (publications, book, EHR Risk Framework) is not owned by that employer.

Research

EHR Risk Framework

Creator and maintainer. Open workbench for longitudinal EHR-style binary risk: index/horizon cohorts, multi-window lookback, patient-level splits, leakage audits, isotonic calibration (Brier, ECE), TreeSHAP, fairness slices, JSON manifests, FastAPI + Angular UI.
