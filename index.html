About

I work two complementary tracks. In research, I study leakage-aware, calibrated, and explainable machine learning for clinical risk and medical imaging, and I maintain open tools other labs can inspect. In engineering, I ship production RAG, multi-step agents, and full-stack platforms at Volkswagen Group of America.

Healthcare methods here are research and education only—not a medical device and not bedside deployment. Industry work is separate from independent publications and the EHR Risk Framework.

Research

Agenda: honest evaluation of EHR and imaging models—index-time feature contracts, patient-level splits, probability calibration, and SHAP explanations—released as papers, a methods book, and citable software.

EHR Risk Framework

Creator and maintainer. Open Python workbench for longitudinal EHR-style binary risk: index time and prediction horizon, multi-window lookback, patient-level splits, leakage audits, isotonic calibration (Brier / ECE), TreeSHAP, fairness slices, JSON run manifests, FastAPI + Angular research UI.

MIT License · research/education prototype · synthetic verification (not clinical performance claims)
