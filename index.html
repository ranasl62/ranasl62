Doctoral research interests

I am interested in a funded Ph.D. focused on trustworthy clinical prediction from real-world health data. Proposed dissertation anchor:

How much do leakage and miscalibration inflate reported performance in EHR risk studies—and can automated index-time audits plus calibration benchmarks reduce that inflation on credentialed cohorts?

  • Temporal integrity / leakage audits for EHR risk models
  • Probability calibration and reliability under shift
  • Open, reproducible research software for multi-site evaluation
